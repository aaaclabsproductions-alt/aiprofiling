AI Profiling

A personal research tool

AI Profiling is a small personal tool. It tracks how the operator's own short videos perform on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and it tests whether a creator's breakout can be predicted early.

It reads view, like, comment and follower counts through each platform's official API. It does this only for accounts the operator owns and has explicitly connected, plus publicly available numbers of public professional accounts where the platform allows it. Every number is stored on the operator's own computer. The tool has no other users, no public sign-up, and it never posts, comments or messages.
