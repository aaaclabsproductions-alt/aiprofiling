Privacy Policy

AI Profiling · last updated 16 November 2026

Who this applies to

AI Profiling is a personal tool run by one person (the "operator") for their own accounts. Nobody else signs up for it or uses it. When you authorize it on YouTube, Instagram/Facebook or TikTok, you do so as the operator, for an account you own.

What data it reads

  • Connected accounts (owned by the operator): the account name and ID; follower, subscriber and video counts; and, per video or post, its publish time, title or caption, duration, and its view, like, comment, share and save counts. It also reads aggregate audience statistics (such as viewer countries or traffic sources) where the platform provides them.
  • Other public accounts: only numbers the platform makes public through its official API for public professional accounts, such as follower counts and per-post view, like and comment counts. It reads no private information about anyone.

It never reads direct messages, comments' authors, contacts, email addresses or payment information. It never posts, edits, deletes, comments or messages on any account.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to measure and compare how videos perform over time, and to make and grade predictions about that performance. It is stored in a database on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored there too, and they are never published.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is not used for advertising or to build profiles of individual people. Summaries the operator chooses to view (for example, a private status page) contain aggregate numbers about the operator's own channels.

Data deletion

To stop the tool reading an account, revoke its access at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook/Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → remove "AI Profiling"
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove "AI Profiling"

To have data that was already collected deleted, open an issue at github.com/aaaclabsproductions-alt/aiprofiling/issues. It will be removed from the local database and its backups within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the project's issue tracker.
